# manual-meter
Manual Meter

A small Windows overlay that shows your controller stick position as a Skate-style circular meter. It works over Roblox and other PC games without changing the game.

How to use

Connect your controller to Windows.

Open ManualMeterCustom.exe.

Move the left stick. The dot inside the circle will move with it.

To use the right stick, right-click the circular meter and choose Read right stick.

Right-click the meter to customise it:

Change the meter colour

Choose an image for the circular background

Choose a solid background colour

Switch between left and right stick

Drag the circle to move it on your screen.

Mouse pass-through

Right-click the meter and choose Mouse pass-through if you want mouse clicks to go through the meter into your game.

To turn it off again, press Ctrl + Shift + M. You can also right-click the Manual Meter icon beside the Windows clock and choose Customise meter.

Notes

The meter stays above your game window. Your colour and background image choices reset when you close the app.
